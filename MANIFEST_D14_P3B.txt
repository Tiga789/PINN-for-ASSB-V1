D14-P3B Batch-5/6 Controlled Replay-Profile Build Smoke
================================================================

Purpose:
  Build a small number of replay-profile NPZ files from XJTU Batch-5 and Batch-6
  raw data to verify that raw time/current/voltage/temperature/cycle/step
  records can be expanded into the GV1 measured-current replay format.

Scope:
  - Batch-5 / Batch-6 only
  - default: 1 file per batch
  - bounded subrecord and point count
  - no training
  - no GV1 mainline modification
  - no SOH generation in voltage soft-label generator
  - no P2D internal-state label generation

Files:
  configs/d14_p3b_batch56_replay_smoke_config.json
  docs/D14_P3B_README.md
  docs/D14_P3B_EXPECTED_OUTPUTS.md
  scripts/gv1_d14_p3b_build_batch56_replay_smoke.py
  scripts/gv1_d14_p3b_verify_outputs.py
  scripts/run_gv1_d14_p3b_batch56_replay_smoke.ps1
