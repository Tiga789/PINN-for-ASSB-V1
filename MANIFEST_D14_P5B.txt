D14-P5B XJTU P2Dlite 8-cell Closed-set Precision Package
=========================================================

Purpose:
  Train a higher-capacity supervised NN on the same 8 P4B-v3 successful
  P2Dlite soft-label profiles and evaluate in closed-set calibration mode.

Boundary:
  - This is not a generalization claim.
  - This does not add new batteries.
  - This does not generate SOH.
  - This does not regenerate soft labels.
  - This does not modify GV1 mainline model/loss/trainer files.

GPU utilization:
  Defaults use GPU-resident tensors, AMP, torch.compile when possible, hidden_dim
  768, 6 layers, and batch_size 65536.
