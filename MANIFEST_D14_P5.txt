D14-P5 XJTU P2Dlite Soft-label Neural-network Smoke Package
=============================================================

Purpose:
  Build, train, evaluate, and verify a small supervised neural-network smoke
  using the eight P4B-v3 successful P2Dlite soft-label profiles.

Scope:
  - supervised NN smoke only;
  - reads P4B-v3 solution_softlabels.npz;
  - predicts theta_a/theta_c/phie/phis_c;
  - n_r=17;
  - no SOH generation;
  - no full-P2D truth claim;
  - no modification of GV1 mainline training code.

Files:
  configs/d14_p5_xjtu_p2dlite_nn_smoke_config.json
  gv1/softlabels_nn/xjtu_p2dlite_dataset.py
  gv1/softlabels_nn/xjtu_p2dlite_model.py
  gv1/softlabels_nn/xjtu_p2dlite_losses.py
  gv1/softlabels_nn/xjtu_p2dlite_metrics.py
  scripts/gv1_d14_p5_build_softlabel_manifest.py
  scripts/gv1_d14_p5_train_p2dlite_softlabel_nn.py
  scripts/gv1_d14_p5_eval_p2dlite_softlabel_nn.py
